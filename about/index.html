About Me

I’m a second-year student interested in software and systems, learning how things work by building small tools and experiments.

Most of the time, I work with Python or C. I enjoy creating things that are practical, functional, or simply interesting enough for someone to find useful in one way or another. I also like automation, organization, and planning.

I’m also beginning to explore areas around system and information security,
mostly through practice and curiosity rather than formal specialization.

This website is a place where I share what I learn, my projects, and sometimes just random thoughts along the way. Feel free to explore my work.

Over time, I’d like to contribute to open-source projects
and grow into more complex systems and software.
